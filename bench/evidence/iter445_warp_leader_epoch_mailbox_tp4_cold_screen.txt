Comparison and decision
-----------------------
The control is stable versus Iteration 432 (73.472 us M8, 300.400 us M128),
so this is not explained by a faster baseline.  Iteration 432's dynamic
named-barrier candidate was 101.056 us M8 and 393.168 us M128.  The epoch
mailbox therefore regresses candidate latency by approximately 18.4% at M8
and 6.3% at M128.  Four warp-leader polling paths, block fences, volatile epoch
traffic, and shuffle broadcast cost more than the removed pre-task named
barrier.  Reject the epoch transport; do not run a five-M formal verdict.
