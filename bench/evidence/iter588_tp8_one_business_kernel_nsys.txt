Conclusion
----------
There is exactly one TP8 business-kernel instance per rank. The eight fill
kernels are the independently issued benchmark cold-L2 clears and are not
part of the timed/business graph. Therefore route prep, W13, activation and
requantization, W2, local k6 combine, and TP all-reduce execute in one CUDA
kernel node on each TP8 rank.

Profiler timing is excluded from performance claims because node tracing
causes large cross-rank instrumentation skew.
