Iteration 570 — native RS two-K64 commit-group experiment, static gate

Date: 2026-09-05

Hypothesis
- The selected register-dequant path issues eight RS-WGMMA instructions for one K128 tile and commits them only after all four K32 operand-dequant chains have run.
- Splitting this into two four-instruction K64 commit groups may let the first group enter the asynchronous GMMA queue while the second K64 operands are prepared, without changing tile bytes, accumulator ownership, activation-scale application, task order, CTA geometry, or communication.

Change
- Added default-off `V4_NATIVE_RS_K64_COMMIT_GROUPS=1`, valid only with register dequant and the selected K128 batching.
- Candidate executes one `warpgroup_commit_batch()` after K32 indices 0–1 and another after indices 2–3, then performs the same final operand fence and `wait_group 0` before reading accumulators.
- Added same-process `rs_k64_commit` TP4 A/B experiment. Both arms retain selected TP-local barriers, route build, parallel combine, weights, routes, CUDA-graph path, and cold-L2 protocol.
