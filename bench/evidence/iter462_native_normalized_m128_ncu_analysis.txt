Interpretation:
- The 8.56% profiled duration reduction tracks the 8.40% instruction-count
  reduction, while occupancy and scheduler starvation are unchanged.  The
  optimization therefore removed real dequant/LUT work rather than changing
  residency or cache warmth.
- The normalized kernel is now more L2/DRAM-utilized, with unchanged L2 hit
  rate, and the arithmetic LUT/global-scale overhead itself is small.
- The old report's original source analysis attributed 1,278 not-issued
  samples to the packed exponent/LUT/word cluster.  The current cluster is
  much smaller; its remaining largest local events are the second exponent
  dependency and the single batched K128 warpgroup wait.
- Cleanup line 698 remains a symptom (dispatch warps waiting for math/combine),
  as proven by Iteration 456.  It is not an independently removable 898-sample
  optimization target.

Decision: retain normalized scale.  A bounded next experiment may pipeline one
next K32 packed/exponent/arithmetic-LUT operand set across RS-WGMMA issue, but
must preserve the 80-register cubin allocation, two-CTA residency and zero
spills.  Do not reattempt coarse cleanup-sync deletion or half-tile prefetch.
